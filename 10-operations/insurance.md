# Business insurance

**Source:** the certificate of liability insurance (`asdf.pdf`, inbox, founder-q3), issued Aug 14, 2025. Policy numbers are left out here on purpose.

- **Broker:** Hollis Agency, Portland, ME (contact: Dean Hollis)
- **Insurer:** Penobscot Mutual Casualty Company

## Coverage, Sep 1, 2025 to Aug 31, 2026

| Coverage | Limit |
|---|---|
| Commercial general liability | $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate |
| Products and completed operations | $2M aggregate |
| Commercial property (contents) | $250,000 |
| Goods in transit / stock throughput | **Not covered** |

## Things to know

- **Fern & Stone is listed as an additional insured** for products we supply to them. New wholesale customers who need this must be listed on the renewal questionnaire.
- The renewal questionnaire is due to the broker **at least 30 days before expiry**, or coverage may lapse.
- Goods in transit aren't covered. Freight damage relies on carrier claims (see [freight](freight-northgate.md)), and losses at the warehouse rely on Tidewater's capped liability (see [fulfillment](fulfillment-tidewater.md)).
