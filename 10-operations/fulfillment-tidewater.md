# Warehousing and fulfillment agreement with Tidewater

**Source:** the signed agreement (`Copy of Copy of terms.pdf`, inbox, founder-q3). It was signed by Rosa Vasquez (General Manager, Tidewater) on Oct 20, 2025 and by Dana Okafor (Founder) on Oct 22, 2025.

It replaces all earlier drafts. A v2 draft with lower fees ($18.00/pallet, $1,400 minimum) is **not** in effect, because the signed version wins.

## What Tidewater does

Tidewater Fulfillment Partners LLC receives, stores, picks, packs and ships our inventory from 4 Riverbend Industrial Park, Biddeford, ME. We get read access to inventory counts through their client portal. **That portal is where live stock levels come from.**

## Fees

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Storage | $19.50 per pallet per month |
| Pick and pack | $2.85 per order + $0.45 per additional unit |
| Receiving | $35.00 per inbound pallet |
| Returns processing | $4.50 per returned order |
| Monthly account minimum | $1,600.00 (initial term) |

- Invoiced monthly after the month ends, payment due in 15 days.
- Tidewater may change fees once per renewal term with 60 days' written notice.

## Term and renewal

- The initial term ran from Nov 1, 2025 to Oct 31, 2026.
- The contract **renews automatically** for 12 months at a time unless either side gives written notice of non-renewal at least **30 days before the term ends**. Late notice doesn't count for the term that's ending.
- **For a renewal starting on or after Nov 1, 2026, the monthly minimum rises to $1,850.00.**
- How to give notice: email contracts@tidewaterfp.example. For non-renewal, email alone is enough. Other notices also need certified mail to the Biddeford address.

> ⚠️ The notice deadline for the first term was **Oct 1, 2026**. There's no record of non-renewal notice being sent. If none was, the contract renewed on Nov 1, 2026 through Oct 31, 2027 at the $1,850 minimum. The next non-renewal deadline would be Oct 1, 2027.

## Liability

- If inventory is lost or damaged, Tidewater pays at most the lesser of our documented cost or $0.50 per pound.
- Tidewater isn't liable for losses under 0.5% of units received in a term.
- The agreement is governed by Maine law.
