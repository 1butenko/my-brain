# Freight carrier, Northgate Freight

**Source:** the Northgate rate quote (`2026 rates.pdf`, inbox, founder-q3).

Northgate Freight is our carrier for palletised shipments that don't fill a whole truck (known as LTL). It handles two routes:

- **Inbound:** stoneware from Kiln & Co in Zanesville, OH
- **Outbound:** shipments from the Tidewater warehouse in Biddeford, ME

## Current quote

- Quote **NGF-Q-4417**, issued Jan 8, 2026, **valid through Dec 31, 2026**. A new quote is needed for 2027.
- Contact: quotes@northgatefreight.example. The full rate table is in the original PDF.
- The quote assumes palletised freight that isn't stacked. Shipments over 8 pallets need a separate one-off quote.
- A fuel surcharge is added weekly based on the national index.

## Things to know

- **Damage found after unpacking (concealed damage) must be claimed within 5 business days of delivery.**
- We have no goods-in-transit insurance (see [insurance](insurance.md)), so freight damage depends on carrier claims.
