# Call with Kiln & Co about the July 2026 stoneware price increase

**Date:** May 12, 2026, 40 min
**Attendees:** Dana (founder), Marcus (ops), Junho (Kiln & Co Ceramics)
**Source:** `notes from call.docx` (inbox, founder-q3)

## Context

Kiln & Co Ceramics in Zanesville, Ohio makes all our stoneware. It is our only source for the speckled glaze.

## What was said

- Clay and freight costs are both up. Kiln & Co is raising prices **14% on all stoneware from the July 2026 run**.
- Junho said he told us about this in February. We have nothing in writing about that.
- At our current wholesale price, this puts the **large serving bowl under 20% margin**. Marcus thinks it's closer to 14%.
- Junho asked again about an outstanding invoice and said their accounts team is chasing it.
- We discussed getting the mugs quoted by a second kiln. Kiln & Co is the only one who can do the speckled glaze, so this is probably not worth it. It was left open.

## Outcomes

- **Decision:** stop selling the large serving bowl once existing stock sells through. Don't reorder it. Keep the small and medium bowls. See [the decision doc](../05-decisions/2026-05-12-drop-large-serving-bowl.md).
