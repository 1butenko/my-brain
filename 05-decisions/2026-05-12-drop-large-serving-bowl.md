# Drop the large serving bowl (STW-BWL-L) after sell-through

**Decided:** May 12, 2026, on the call with Kiln & Co
**Decided by:** Dana (founder) and Marcus (ops)
**Source:** [meeting notes](../06-meetings/2026-05-12-kiln-and-co-price-increase.md)

## Decision

We stop selling the large speckled serving bowl (STW-BWL-L) once existing stock sells through. We do **not** reorder it. The small and medium bowls stay in the range.

## Why

Kiln & Co is raising stoneware prices 14% from the July 2026 production run. At our current wholesale price of $44.00, that leaves the large bowl under 20% margin. Marcus estimates it's closer to 14%. That's too thin to keep it in the range.

## Alternatives considered

- **Get it made by a second kiln.** This came up for the mugs. Kiln & Co is the only maker who can do the speckled glaze, so a second kiln wouldn't give us a matching product. Judged probably not worth it.
- Raising the wholesale price was not recorded as discussed.

## Consequences

- The [Spring 2026 line sheet](../07-pitch/2026-spring-wholesale-line-sheet.md) still lists the large bowl. **That listing is out of date.** Don't include the bowl in future line sheets.
- Fern & Stone, our largest wholesale account, had been shown the bowl in the March line sheet. In April they asked whether it was still available at that price. Dana was to tell them before they built their fall assortment. Whether that happened is not recorded.
